Our programs

How we work

Six interconnected pathways — each designed to meet youth where they are and take them where they want to go.

Flagship

Innovate Nairobi Bootcamp

An intensive 8-week programme in digital skills, business planning, and startup fundamentals — culminating in a live pitch to investors.

Ongoing

Hustle Hub Mentorship

A year-round mentorship circle pairing emerging entrepreneurs with seasoned business leaders for hands-on guidance and accountability.

Grants

Micro-Enterprise Fund

Seed grants of KES 10,000–50,000 for youth-led ventures that pass our business viability review — no collateral required.

Schools

Youth in Business

A schools-based financial literacy and entrepreneurship curriculum reaching secondary students in Nairobi's underserved estates.

Women-led

Mama Inua

Targeted support for young women entrepreneurs — combining training, peer networks, and access to women-focused financing.

Tech

Code & Create

Practical digital skills — coding, design, and freelancing — enabling youth to access the global digital economy from Nairobi.
