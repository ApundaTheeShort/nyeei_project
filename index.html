Our mission

Economic power
in young hands

We believe Nairobi's youth are not the leaders of tomorrow — they are the leaders of today. Our target: reach 300,000 young people across all 17 constituencies and give them the tools to prove it.

Skills training

Practical, market-relevant skills in technology, finance, and entrepreneurship — taught by industry professionals.

Mentorship

One-on-one connections with successful entrepreneurs and business leaders who guide youth from idea to execution.

Access to capital

Micro-grants, pitch competitions, and financial literacy programs that help youth secure the funding to launch.

Community roots

Programmes run within Nairobi's neighbourhoods — building local economic ecosystems that stay and grow.
