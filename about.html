Founder Spotlight

John Ochieng Ohuru

Founder & Chief Executive Officer
Economist John Ochieng Ohuru founded NYEEI with a firm conviction: that Nairobi's youth possess the talent, energy, and business vision to drive Kenya's economic future—they simply require structured opportunity, capital access, and guidance. Drawing on his experience in economic analysis and community development, John built NYEEI from the ground up to connect 300,000 young leaders across all 17 constituencies with the tools to succeed.
"Effective youth empowerment is not charity—it is a strategic investment in human capability, community resilience, and economic sovereignty."
🎓 Economist 🏛️ Founder & CEO 🇰🇪 Community Builder
