Leadership

The team behind NYEEI

A passionate, multidisciplinary team united by one goal — economic empowerment for every young Nairobian.

JO
John Ochieng Ohuru
Founder & CEO
Economist John Ochieng Ohuru founded NYEEI with a single conviction: that Nairobi's youth have the talent, drive, and ideas to transform Kenya's economy — they simply need access, opportunity, and belief. Drawing on his background in economics and community development, John built NYEEI from the ground up to bridge the gap between potential and prosperity for thousands of young Nairobians.
🎓 Economist  ·  Founder  ·  Youth Advocate
MO
CPA Moses Odhiambo Okal
Director of Partnerships & Resource Mobilization
CPA Moses Odhiambo Okal leads NYEEI's efforts to forge strategic alliances with corporates, development organisations, and government agencies that share our vision. A Certified Public Accountant with a background in stakeholder engagement and fundraising, he has helped NYEEI grow its partner network and secure the resources needed to scale programmes across Nairobi.
"I was inspired by seeing brilliant young people in my community held back not by lack of ambition, but by lack of opportunity. At NYEEI, we close that gap — one partnership at a time."
GO
George Owino
Director of Programs & Projects
George Owino oversees the design, delivery, and quality of all NYEEI programmes — from the Innovate Nairobi Bootcamp to the Youth in Business schools curriculum. A seasoned programme manager, he ensures that every initiative is grounded in real community needs and delivers measurable outcomes for the youth we serve.
"Growing up, I watched my peers drop out of the system with no support. I joined NYEEI because I believe structured, well-designed programmes can change the trajectory of an entire generation."
JM
Joseph Omondi
Director of Finance & Administration
Joseph is the financial backbone of NYEEI, ensuring every donor contribution is managed with the highest standards of transparency and accountability. He oversees budgeting, reporting, and organisational compliance — giving our partners and donors full confidence that their investment reaches the youth it is meant for.
"Sound financial management is not just about numbers — it is about trust. When donors trust us, more young people get funded. That is what drives me every day."
LO
Lawrence Ojiambo
Director of Communications & Innovation
Lawrence shapes how NYEEI tells its story to the world. He leads communications strategy, brand development, and the integration of innovative tools that amplify the voices of our youth. His work ensures that the remarkable things happening in our programmes reach the audiences — donors, partners, and policymakers — who need to hear them.
"Innovation is not just about technology — it is about finding new ways to make people care. I joined NYEEI because here, every story we tell has the power to change a life."
BW
Bon Wise Frank Ouru
Tech Expert
Bon Wise is the technological engine behind NYEEI's digital infrastructure. From the organisation's online platforms to the Code & Create programme, he ensures that technology is both a tool for delivery and a skill we pass on to youth. He is passionate about making the digital economy accessible to young Nairobians who have historically been left behind.
"Tech is the great equaliser — but only if young people have access to it. I am here to make sure no Nairobi youth is locked out of the digital world because of where they were born."
RP
Rowzil Paultine
Director of Gender Inclusion & Community Engagement
Rowzil champions equity at the heart of everything NYEEI does. She leads the Mama Inua programme for young women entrepreneurs and ensures that all NYEEI initiatives are designed with gender sensitivity and community ownership in mind. Her work builds the bridges between NYEEI and the communities we serve, making sure no young person is left behind.
"I was inspired by the women in my community who had the drive to build businesses but were never given a seat at the table. At NYEEI, we do not just give them a seat — we help them build the table."
DO
Danrai Otieno
Director of Youth Mobilization & Civic Engagement
Danrai Otieno brings the power of grassroots youth leadership to NYEEI. As President of the Young Youth Alliance, he has built an extensive network of young civic champions across Nairobi, mobilising communities from the ground up. At NYEEI, he drives youth outreach, civic education, and the grassroots campaigns that bring our programmes to the 300,000 young people we are committed to reaching across all 17 constituencies.
"Young people are not waiting to be given power — they are already powerful. My role at NYEEI is to channel that power into opportunities that transform lives and communities."

Advisory Council

RL
Raphael Lusi Oguk
Chairperson, Advisory Council
Raphael Lusi Oguk is a distinguished student leader and institutional builder who brings unparalleled credibility and networks to NYEEI's advisory function. As Secretary General Emeritus at the Technical University of Kenya, he helped shape one of Kenya's most active student governance structures. He is the Founder and former President of LCSA, and brings an extensive, established network of young leaders, academics, and institutions that strengthens NYEEI's reach and impact across Nairobi and beyond.
🎓 Secretary General Emeritus — TUK
🏛️ Founder & Former President — LCSA
🤝 Established Youth Network
"The greatest investment any society can make is in its youth. At NYEEI, I am proud to lend my experience and networks to ensure that Nairobi's young people have the platform, the guidance, and the opportunity they deserve."
